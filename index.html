About Us

Welcome to Bigcarz

🧭 Who We Are

We are a passionate travel company dedicated to creating safe, comfortable, and memorable road journeys across India. From scenic highways to sacred destinations, we specialize in customized tour packages designed for families, friends, corporate groups, and solo travelers.

🚐 What We Do

We offer well-planned travel packages using cars, buses, and tempo travellers, covering popular tourist destinations, pilgrimage routes, weekend getaways, and long-distance road trips. Every journey is carefully designed to match your comfort, schedule, and travel preferences.

🛡️ Safety & Comfort First (Very Important)

Your safety is our top priority. All our vehicles are regularly serviced, well-maintained, and driven by experienced, licensed drivers who understand Indian roads inside and out. We follow strict safety standards to ensure a smooth and worry-free travel experience.

Services

Our Services

Pan-India Road Tours/All-India Road Trip Packages

Explore Incredible India by road with our Pan-India tour packages. From scenic highways to cultural landmarks, we offer safe, comfortable, and fully customized road trips across the country—designed for families, groups, and travelers who value reliability and premium service.

Hotel Reservations (On Request)

We offer hotel reservations as an optional service to complement your road journey. Based on your destination, travel style, and budget, we arrange clean, comfortable, and carefully selected hotels to ensure a relaxing stay after every day of travel. Whether you prefer budget-friendly comfort or premium accommodations, we partner with trusted hotels across India to provide safe and pleasant stays—only when you choose to include them.

Travel suggestion

Not sure where to go or how to plan your route? Our travel suggestions are thoughtfully curated to help you discover the best destinations across India based on season, road conditions, travel duration, and group preferences. From scenic road trips and cultural circuits to spiritual journeys and weekend escapes, we guide you toward destinations that offer comfort, safety, and memorable experiences. Whether you’re planning a short getaway or an extended road tour, we ensure you travel smarter, avoid common hassles, and make the most of every mile on the road.

Trip Planning

Every journey is unique, and so is the way we plan it. Our trip planning service is focused on creating personalized road travel experiences across India, tailored to your destinations, travel duration, group size, and comfort preferences. From route planning and vehicle selection to travel timing and rest stops, we take care of every detail to ensure a smooth and stress-free journey. Whether you’re planning a family vacation, pilgrimage, corporate trip, or long-distance road tour, our experienced team works closely with you to design an itinerary that balances comfort, safety, and memorable experiences—so you can travel with complete confidence.
